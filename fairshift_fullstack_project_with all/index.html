Core Capabilities

Everything needed for valid, transparent, and stable seat allocation under changing conditions.

🧮

Constraint Optimization

Google OR-Tools CP-SAT models eligibility, capacities, ranked preferences, and existing assignments to produce optimal or high-quality feasible solutions.

🔄

Minimal Disruption Reassignment

When capacities change or applicants withdraw, the engine recomputes allocations while preserving as many valid existing placements as possible.

🔍

Counterfactual Appeals

Evaluate any requested placement by comparing the baseline solution with an alternative that accommodates the request, quantifying effects on others.

✅

Independent Validation

A separate validation layer verifies every assignment against declared rules and clearly distinguishes proven infeasibility from timeouts or unresolved outcomes.

📜

Full Audit Trail

Every allocation change, capacity update, and appeal decision is logged with timestamps, solver status, and human-readable explanations.

📊

Transparent Metrics

Preference satisfaction, reassignment counts, affected applicants, and constraint compliance are displayed for every solution.

How FAIRSHIFT Works

01

Model the Problem

Applicants, programs, eligibility rules, seat capacities, ranked preferences, and existing assignments are encoded as a formal constraint optimization model.

02

Solve with CP-SAT

The solver finds an assignment that satisfies all hard constraints while maximizing preference satisfaction and minimizing disruption to confirmed placements.

03

Handle Changes

On withdrawals or capacity updates the engine re-solves, identifying exactly which assignments must change and which can remain stable.

04

Evaluate Appeals

Counterfactual analysis shows the impact of granting a request on other applicants, total preference score, and overall feasibility.
